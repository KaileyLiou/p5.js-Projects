Animal Rights:

Made By: Kailey

Animals deserve to be treated equally. Animal rights are important because many animals around the world are suffering from being used for humans' entertainment, food, fashion, medicine, etc.

Some basic animal rights are that animals may not be used for food, cannot be hunted, their habitats must be protected, etc.

There are more than 11 million animal rights activists. Some famous ones include: Jane Goodall, Caroline Earle White, Margaret Murie, Velma Johnston, Cesar Chavez, Henry Spira, and a lot more.


Animals are currently being...

  • Tested and getting injured
  • Crammed together with not much space
  • Getting drugged, killed, and eaten
  • Used in experiments related to
    • getting seperated from their families
    • being blind
    • dying
  • And a lot more

Some ways you can help include:

  1. Purchase cruelty-free items
  2. Donate to charities
  3. Educate yourself and others
  4. Spread the issue!
